## Best way to show the client (5 minutes)
1. Open the website `/` → click **Book a table** → choose a table in 3D.
2. On a phone, open `/room/ceylon-ayu/101` → order a dish → send a towel request.
3. On a laptop, sign in as **Chef** → Kitchen display → **Start cooking** → **Ready**.
4. **Try another role → Waiter** → Waiter service board: the dish is waiting with a chime → **Served**. It is now on room 101’s bill.
5. **Try another role → Receptionist** → Guest billing → room 101 → see the charge, print the bill and registration card.
6. **Owner** → Dashboard, Channel manager, Parking, Roles & permissions.
Tip: open steps 2–4 in tabs of the **same browser** — they update each other live.
